# Minimum Requirements
These are the lowest specs where CodePilot will run, but performance may be slow with larger models.

CPU: Quad‑core (Intel i5 8th gen / Ryzen 3 3000 series)

RAM: 16 GB

GPU: None required (CPU inference supported)

Storage: 100 GB free space

Local AI models take significant storage

Multiple models + Unity project + CodePilot tools

OS: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64‑bit)

Unity Version: Unity 2022.3+

Supported Models: Qwen 2.5 Coder 7B, DeepSeek 7B

-------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recommended Requirements
This is exactly your PC — the ideal setup for smooth performance.

CPU: Intel i5‑12600KF / Ryzen 5 5600X or better

RAM: 32 GB

GPU: GTX 1050 Ti or better (optional, not required)

Storage: 100–200 GB free space

Enough for multiple 7B–33B models

OS: Windows 11

Unity Version: Unity 2022.3 LTS or 2023 LTS

Supported Models:

Qwen 2.5 Coder 7B / 14B / 32B

DeepSeek LLM 7B / 33B

# High‑End Requirements (Optional)

CPU: Ryzen 7 / Intel i7 or better

Storage: 200–400 GB free space
